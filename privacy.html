Privacy policy

rclone Drive Backup · last updated 1 November 2026

What data is accessed

The tool reads the files in my own Google Drive account, using permission I granted through Google's sign-in screen.

Where it goes

Files are copied only to a hard drive on my own home server. They are not sent to any other server, person or company, and nothing is sold, shared or used for advertising.

Other people's data

The tool accesses no one else's account. Files that others have shared into my Drive are stored privately on my own drive, along with the rest of my backup.

Revoking access

Access can be removed at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.
